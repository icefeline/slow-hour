SLOW GARDEN — HOW "WHAT THIS COULD MEAN FOR YOU" IS WRITTEN
The personalised reading on the card detail, generated once per card per day.
Source: app/api/calculate-transit/route.ts, lib/utils/vedic-signal.ts,
        lib/utils/jyotish.ts, lib/utils/astrology-calculator.ts
Exported 2026-08-14.

==============================================================================
SHORT VERSION
==============================================================================

The words are written by Claude (claude-haiku-4-5, max 500 tokens), returning
four fields as JSON. Everything it is given is computed from a real ephemeris
or written by hand. Which card you drew is NOT chosen by AI — it is a hash of
the date.

==============================================================================
STEP 0 — THE CARD ITSELF (no AI)
==============================================================================

  card       = seeded hash of the date (+ birth date), over the 78-card deck
  reversed   = a second hash of the same seed
  meaning    = looked up verbatim from the hand-written deck

Same person, same day, same card, every time. Re-opening does not re-roll.

==============================================================================
STEP 1 — THE CHART (no AI)
==============================================================================

Same calculation as the welcome message: Nominatim for coordinates, the birth
place's timezone for the true instant, astronomy-engine for positions, Lahiri
ayanamsa for sidereal, Whole Sign houses, mean lunar node for Rahu/Ketu.

==============================================================================
STEP 2 — WHAT IS LIVE IN THE CHART TODAY (no AI)
==============================================================================

Four layers are computed, each moving at a different speed. Every one is
deterministic — no model decides what is active.

  DASHA        the Vimshottari period the person is living through. The first
               mahadasha is the janma nakshatra's lord, already part spent at
               birth by the fraction of the nakshatra the Moon had crossed;
               the rest follow the fixed 120-year order. The running
               mahadasha and antardasha are both computed, with how far
               through each one the person is.
               Scale: years, then months.

  GOCHARA      where each graha sits today counted from the janma rashi,
               1-12, and whether that position is classically favourable for
               that graha. Saturn over the 12th, 1st or 2nd from the Moon is
               named as sade sati, with its phase (rising / peak / setting).
               Scale: weeks.

  DRISHTI      which grahas are looking at which natal grahas today. Whole
               sign glances, not orb-based aspects: everything aspects the
               7th, Mars also 4/7/8, Jupiter 5/7/9, Saturn 3/7/10, the nodes
               5/7/9.
               Scale: days.

  DIGNITY      whether today's leading graha is exalted, debilitated, in its
               own sign or neutral; whether it is combust (too close to the
               Sun); whether it is retrograde; and its navamsa sign.
               Flavour on top.

Plus RETURNS: any graha within 3 degrees of where it stood at birth.

WHICH ONE LEADS
  Every live signal is scored: base strength, +10 if the graha matches the
  drawn card's affinity, +5 if it also rules the current dasha, plus a
  deterministic jitter seeded from the day so the same person on the same day
  always gets the same lead, but an ordinary week moves around the chart.
  The dasha is deliberately excluded from this contest — it is reported
  separately as the chapter every day, and it outscores everything else by
  construction, so leading with it would name the same planet daily.

==============================================================================
STEP 3 — THE LAYERS BECOME PLAIN ENGLISH (no AI)
==============================================================================

No jargon reaches the model. Each graha and each house has a hand-written
line, so nothing technical can leak into the reading:

  sun      "the part of you that wants to be met on its own terms"
  moon     "the part that feels first and explains itself later"
  mercury  "the part that needs to name a thing before it can put it down"
  venus    "the part that reaches for beauty, ease, and being wanted"
  mars     "the part that pushes, and knows what it is willing to fight for"
  jupiter  "the part that widens, believes, and looks for the meaning in it"
  saturn   "the part that keeps time, holds the line, and asks what is
            actually real"
  rahu     "the hunger that pulls forward into unfamiliar ground"
  ketu     "the part already letting go, and quietly bored of what used to
            work"

The twelve houses from the Moon are written the same way, e.g.
  6th  "the daily grind and what it is wearing down"
  10th "the work, and being watched while doing it"
  12th "the inner room, solitude, and what is being released"

The assembled block looks like this:

  Chapter (ending): a long stretch about meaning, growth, and what is
  believed.
  Sub-chapter (ending): the hunger that pulls forward into unfamiliar ground.
  Today's strongest thread: the part that keeps time, holds the line, and
  asks what is actually real is sitting over what they are holding onto, and
  what it costs to keep, where it tends to chafe.
  It is moving backwards — revisiting rather than advancing.
  Also live, quieter:
  - ...
  - ...

==============================================================================
STEP 4 — CONTINUITY
==============================================================================

Up to 10 previous memoryNotes and 10 recent card names are included, with an
explicit instruction never to reference them aloud. This is what makes a run
of readings feel continuous without the app saying "as I noticed last week".

==============================================================================
STEP 5 — THE PROMPT
==============================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[memory block, only if there is history]
Background on this person — use this only to deepen your understanding of
their current arc. Do not reference it directly. Do not say "as I've
noticed", "you've been dealing with", or anything that signals you remember
past readings. Let it quietly inform your accuracy and sensitivity.

Recent patterns:
- ...
Cards drawn recently: ...

[main prompt]
You write the personalised synthesis for a tarot app called Slow Garden. This
is the "what this could mean for you" section — the part that makes the
reading feel like it was written specifically for this person.

The app exists to help people see themselves more clearly, not to tell them
what to do or think. The best insight doesn't hand them a conclusion — it
holds up a mirror at exactly the right angle so they can find the thing
themselves. Think less therapist, more that one friend who says the true
thing gently and then lets you sit with it.

Astrology context: Vedic sidereal zodiac (Lahiri ayanamsa), Whole Sign houses.

Voice (these matter):
- Entirely lowercase, no em dashes
- Sound like a perceptive friend, not a practitioner
- Be specific — vague wisdom is worthless here
- No "journey", "navigate", "embrace", "explore", "tap into", "delve",
  "unpack", "honour your", "give yourself permission"
- No questions anywhere in the insight or action (the card already asks them)
- Do not restate or paraphrase the card meaning — they can read it
- Do not explain the transit in general terms — tell them what THIS transit
  means in THEIR life right now
- Do not name any zodiac sign

[data note, when birth time or location is missing]

About this person:
- Janma nakshatra: ${nakshatraName} — fixed at birth, the deepest layer of
  how they are built
- Moon sign / janma rashi (sidereal): ${moonSign} — in Jyotish this is the
  primary sign, how they actually feel and process
- Sun sign (sidereal): ${sunSign} — secondary here; the outward-facing self,
  not the core
- Card drawn: ${cardName} (${orientation})
- Card themes: ${coreThemes}
- Emotional tone of this card: ${emotionalTone}

What's happening in their chart right now.

This is read the Jyotish way, in layers that move at different speeds: the
dasha is the chapter they are years into, the gochara is where the grahas are
sitting relative to their birth Moon, and the drishti is what has its
attention on what today. The chapter is the backdrop; the strongest thread is
the foreground. Let the two speak to each other — a small daily pressure
inside a long chapter reads very differently from the same pressure inside a
different one.

${vedicContext}   <- the plain-English block from step 3

Supporting western-style reading of the same sky, use only if it adds
something:
- ${transitingPlanet} ${aspectType} ${natalPlanet}, ${phase}
- landing in the ${house}th house: ${houseTheme}

Write a JSON object with exactly these four fields:

"keyPhrase": 3-6 words. The headline for this specific moment — something
that could only come from the combination of this card and this transit. Not
a platitude. Lowercase.

"insight": 2-4 sentences. This is the main reading. The card is a pattern.
The transit is a pressure or an opening. The house is where it's playing out
in their life. Put those three things together — not as separate facts, but
as one coherent thing that's happening to them right now. Let their sun sign
quietly shape the flavour of how they experience it. Make them feel seen in a
way that surprises them slightly. No moralising, no advice. No questions.

"action": 1-2 sentences. Not a task — a practice of noticing. Help them catch
themselves at the exact moment the pattern runs automatically: the flinch,
the deflection, the story they tell to stay comfortable. Frame it as an
observation to collect, not a behaviour to change. The awareness is the whole
point.

"memoryNote": One sentence, lowercase. An honest private note about what's
active in this person's inner life right now — written as a theme, not an
event. No card names, no planet names, no "they" or "you". Pattern language
only ("tension between X and Y" or "themes of A surfacing alongside B").

Return only valid JSON, no markdown, no extra text.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

==============================================================================
STEP 6 — WHAT COMES BACK
==============================================================================

  keyPhrase   3-6 word headline
  insight     the reading itself, 2-4 sentences
  action      the "try this" noticing practice
  memoryNote  private, never shown; stored locally and fed into future
              readings for continuity

If the model returns nothing usable, the app shows an error rather than
substituting a generic reading.

==============================================================================
WHAT IS AND IS NOT AI
==============================================================================

  no AI       which card, whether it is reversed, the card's meaning, the
              whole chart, the dasha, the gochara, the drishti, the dignity,
              which signal leads today, and every description above
  AI          keyPhrase, insight, action, memoryNote

Personalisation off = no request is made at all. The reader gets the card and
its traditional meaning.

==============================================================================
KNOWN LIMITS
==============================================================================

  · Vimshottari runs to mahadasha and antardasha only — no deeper levels.
  · No vargas beyond the navamsa, which is computed but not yet read.
  · No yogas.
  · The supporting "western-style" line still uses Uranus, Neptune and Pluto,
    which classical Jyotish does not recognise. It is secondary to the Vedic
    block but it is still in the prompt.
