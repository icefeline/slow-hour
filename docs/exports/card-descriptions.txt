MAJOR
——————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

THE FOOL  [major-0]
  The Fool is the first card of the Major Arcana and represents the
  beginning of a journey.

THE MAGICIAN  [major-1]
  The Magician harnesses the power of the universe to manifest desires into
  reality.

THE HIGH PRIESTESS  [major-2]
  The High Priestess sits at the gate between the conscious and subconscious
  realms.

THE EMPRESS  [major-3]
  The Empress embodies the archetypal mother figure, representing creation
  and abundance.

THE EMPEROR  [major-4]
  The Emperor represents masculine energy, structure, and the establishment
  of order.

THE HIEROPHANT  [major-5]
  The Hierophant represents traditional values and sacred rituals.

THE LOVERS  [major-6]
  The Lovers represents union, relationships, and the choices we make in
  love.

THE CHARIOT  [major-7]
  The Chariot represents triumph over obstacles through determination and
  control.

STRENGTH  [major-8]
  Strength represents the quiet power of compassion and inner fortitude.

THE HERMIT  [major-9]
  The Hermit represents the path of introspection and seeking inner truth.

WHEEL OF FORTUNE  [major-10]
  The Wheel of Fortune represents the ever-turning cycles of life and fate.

JUSTICE  [major-11]
  Justice represents truth, fairness, and the law of cause and effect.

THE HANGED MAN  [major-12]
  The Hanged Man represents surrender and the wisdom gained through new
  perspectives.

DEATH  [major-13]
  Death represents profound transformation and the necessary endings that
  make way for new beginnings.

TEMPERANCE  [major-14]
  Temperance represents the alchemical process of blending opposites into
  wholeness.

THE DEVIL  [major-15]
  The Devil represents the chains we forge ourselves and the illusions that
  bind us.

THE TOWER  [major-16]
  The Tower represents sudden upheaval and the liberation that comes from
  necessary destruction.

THE STAR  [major-17]
  The Star represents the return of hope and the healing light that guides
  us forward.

THE MOON  [major-18]
  The Moon represents the realm of illusion, intuition, and the mysteries of
  the subconscious.

THE SUN  [major-19]
  The Sun represents the radiant energy of joy, success, and unfiltered
  vitality.

JUDGEMENT  [major-20]
  Judgement represents the moment of awakening and the call to rise into a
  new version of yourself.

THE WORLD  [major-21]
  The World represents the culmination of a journey and the integration of
  all you have learned.

CUPS
——————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

ACE OF CUPS  [cups-ace]
  The Ace of Cups represents the pure potential of emotional and creative
  abundance.

TWO OF CUPS  [cups-2]
  The Two of Cups represents the sacred bond of mutual respect and emotional
  connection.

THREE OF CUPS  [cups-3]
  The Three of Cups represents the joy found in authentic community and
  shared celebration.

FOUR OF CUPS  [cups-4]
  The Four of Cups represents the danger of apathy and the invitation to see
  what is already being offered.

FIVE OF CUPS  [cups-5]
  The Five of Cups represents the grief of loss and the choice to see what
  still remains.

SIX OF CUPS  [cups-6]
  The Six of Cups represents the tender gifts of memory and the healing
  found in innocence.

SEVEN OF CUPS  [cups-7]
  The Seven of Cups represents the dizzying array of choices and the need
  for discernment.

EIGHT OF CUPS  [cups-8]
  The Eight of Cups represents the brave journey of leaving behind what no
  longer serves the soul.

NINE OF CUPS  [cups-9]
  The Nine of Cups represents the deep satisfaction of wishes fulfilled and
  contentment earned.

TEN OF CUPS  [cups-10]
  The Ten of Cups represents the culmination of emotional fulfillment and
  lasting harmony.

PAGE OF CUPS  [cups-page]
  The Page of Cups represents the tender beginnings of emotional and
  creative exploration.

KNIGHT OF CUPS  [cups-knight]
  The Knight of Cups represents the courage to follow what makes your heart
  come alive.

QUEEN OF CUPS  [cups-queen]
  The Queen of Cups represents the power of emotional wisdom and
  compassionate presence.

KING OF CUPS  [cups-king]
  The King of Cups represents the wisdom of emotional mastery and
  compassionate leadership.

PENTACLES
——————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————————

ACE OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-ace]
  The Ace of Pentacles represents the seed of material abundance and the
  potential for grounded growth.

TWO OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-2]
  The Two of Pentacles represents the dynamic balance of managing multiple
  responsibilities.

THREE OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-3]
  The Three of Pentacles represents the power of collaboration and the
  mastery built through shared effort.

FOUR OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-4]
  The Four of Pentacles represents the tension between security and the fear
  of scarcity.

FIVE OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-5]
  The Five of Pentacles represents the pain of hardship and the help we
  sometimes cannot see.

SIX OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-6]
  The Six of Pentacles represents the flow of generosity and the balance of
  giving and receiving.

SEVEN OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-7]
  The Seven of Pentacles represents the patience required between effort and
  reward.

EIGHT OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-8]
  The Eight of Pentacles represents the dedication required to achieve
  mastery through practice.

NINE OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-9]
  The Nine of Pentacles represents the rewards of self-sufficiency and the
  pleasure of independence.

TEN OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-10]
  The Ten of Pentacles represents the culmination of wealth and the legacy
  we leave behind.

PAGE OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-page]
  The Page of Pentacles represents the grounded curiosity of learning and
  the patience of new beginnings.

KNIGHT OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-knight]
  The Knight of Pentacles represents the steady dedication of consistent
  effort and reliable progress.

QUEEN OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-queen]
  The Queen of Pentacles represents the generosity of nurturing and the
  abundance of grounded care.

KING OF PENTACLES  [pentacles-king]
  The King of Pentacles represents the mastery of material abundance and the
  wisdom of grounded leadership.
