SLOW GARDEN — HOW THE WELCOME MESSAGE IS WRITTEN
The message shown once, at the end of onboarding, before the first draw.
Source: app/api/welcome-insight/route.ts
Exported 2026-08-14.

==============================================================================
SHORT VERSION
==============================================================================

The words are written by Claude (claude-haiku-4-5, max 220 tokens).
Everything Claude is given is computed or hand-written first — it never sees
a birth date, and it is never asked to do astrology. It is given four
pre-written descriptions and a strict voice spec, and asked to join them up.

==============================================================================
STEP 1 — WHAT THE PERSON GIVES
==============================================================================

  name            required (falls back to "friend")
  birth date      required, DD/MM/YYYY
  birth time      optional
  birth location  optional

Anything longer than its limit is truncated and stripped:
name 100 chars, location 200, time 5.

==============================================================================
STEP 2 — THE CHART IS CALCULATED (no AI)
==============================================================================

calculateNatalChart() runs before any model call:

  · birth location -> coordinates via OpenStreetMap Nominatim
  · coordinates -> IANA timezone, and the birth clock time is resolved to a
    real UTC instant in that zone (historical DST included)
  · planetary positions from the astronomy-engine ephemeris
  · Lahiri ayanamsa subtracted -> sidereal positions
  · sidereal ascendant -> Whole Sign houses
  · Rahu and Ketu from the mean lunar node

From that chart three things are pulled:

  JANMA NAKSHATRA  the nakshatra the Moon occupied at birth, from the real
                   sidereal Moon. 27 possibilities.
  JANMA RASHI      the sidereal Moon sign. 12 possibilities.
  LIFE PATH        the birth date's digits summed to a single digit, keeping
                   11/22/33. This is numerology, not astrology.

==============================================================================
STEP 3 — THOSE THREE BECOME HAND-WRITTEN TEXT
==============================================================================

Each lookup is a phrase written by hand and stored in the route. Claude is
given the phrase, never the name of the placement.

  · 27 nakshatra quality lines — one per nakshatra
      e.g. dhanishtha: "rhythm and abundance — moves between worlds easily,
      gathers and gives in equal measure, a certain restless vitality"

  · 9 dasha theme lines — one per nakshatra ruling planet
      e.g. mars: "a drive toward action and will — what is worth fighting
      for, what isn't"

  · 12 moon rashi quality lines — one per sign
      e.g. aquarius: "needs distance to understand what it feels — raw
      emotion without analysis is overwhelming"

  · 12 life path notes — one per number
      e.g. 9: "a path of release — always finishing cycles before others are
      ready for them to end"

A greeting is also chosen by the nakshatra's ruling planet, from a fixed
menu of two per planet:

  ketu     "{name}." / "oh, {name}."
  venus    "welcome, {name}." / "hey, {name}."
  sun      "hey, {name}." / "{name}."
  moon     "hey, {name}." / "oh, {name}."
  mars     "hey, {name}." / "there you are, {name}."
  rahu     "oh, {name}." / "there you are, {name}."
  jupiter  "welcome, {name}." / "good, {name}."
  saturn   "{name}." / "good, {name}."
  mercury  "hey, {name}." / "welcome, {name}."

==============================================================================
STEP 4 — HONESTY ABOUT PRECISION
==============================================================================

One of four data notes is included, so the model knows what it can claim:

  time + location   "full Jyotish context — janma nakshatra, moon rashi and
                    lagna all exact"
  time only         "janma nakshatra and moon rashi exact; lagna approximate
                    without location"
  neither, Moon stayed in one nakshatra all day
                    "janma nakshatra and moon rashi are still exact; no lagna"
  neither, Moon crossed a boundary that day
                    "treat the janma nakshatra as the likelier of two; no lagna"

==============================================================================
STEP 5 — THE PROMPT
==============================================================================

Sent verbatim, with ${...} filled from the steps above.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You write the welcome message for Slow Garden, a tarot app grounded in Vedic
astrology (Jyotish). Someone has just entered their birth details for the
first time. This is what they read first — a brief, personal reflection that
shows their chart has actually been considered, and opens toward their first
card draw.

This app uses Jyotish, not Western astrology. That means:
- The janma nakshatra (birth lunar mansion) is the most personal and
  distinctive marker — lean into it heavily
- The janma rashi (moon sign in Jyotish) is the primary identity marker —
  more important than the sun sign
- Do NOT write about sun sign energy as the main thing — the nakshatra and
  moon rashi say more
- The ruling planet of the nakshatra colours what the soul is being asked to
  work with

Voice (non-negotiable):
- Entirely lowercase, no em dashes, no exclamation marks
- Perceptive friend who just looked at the chart — warm and specific, not
  practitioner-speak
- Begin with this greeting (choose the one that best fits the nakshatra
  energy): ${greetingHint}
- After the greeting: 3 sentences only. Each must open with a different word.
  No sentence after the greeting may start with "you"
- Use varied openers: "there's", "something in", "a lot of what", "for
  someone", "in people with", "what shows up", "the pattern", "most of what",
  "some of", "this kind of"
- Be specific to the actual placements — not generic. The nakshatra quality
  is the anchor
- Final sentence: 5-8 words, quietly gestures toward the card draw
- No nakshatra name, no rashi name, no planet name — describe the quality,
  embody the concept
- No: "journey", "navigate", "embrace", "explore", "tap into", "delve",
  "unpack", "honour", "karma", "dharma", "resonate"
- No questions

About this person:

Janma nakshatra quality: ${nakshatra.quality}
Nakshatra ruling planet theme: ${DASHA_THEMES[nakshatra.planet]}
Janma rashi (moon sign) quality: ${MOON_RASHI_QUALITIES[moonRashi]}
Life path: ${LIFE_PATH_NOTES[lifePath]}
Data: ${dataNote}

Write: greeting line + exactly 3 sentences. Total ~65-80 words. Nothing else.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

==============================================================================
WHAT IS AND IS NOT AI
==============================================================================

  computed, no AI    the chart, the nakshatra, the rashi, the life path,
                     the greeting menu, all four description sets
  written by AI      the 4 sentences the reader sees

If the reader turned personalisation off during onboarding, no request is
made at all and a local message is used instead.
