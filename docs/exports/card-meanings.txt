SLOW GARDEN — CARD MEANINGS
Every card's upright and reversed meaning, exactly as shown under "meaning"
on the card detail. 78 cards. Exported 2026-08-14.

These are hand-written and are never generated: the app looks them up verbatim.

THE FOOL  [major-0]

  UPRIGHT
  There is no map here, no guarantee of safety, only the wild call of
  possibility. Where in your life are you being called to begin again, to
  step toward something that excites and terrifies you in equal measure?
  What would you do today if you trusted that the ground would rise to meet
  you? What part of you remembers how to trust the fall?

  keywords: new beginnings, innocence, spontaneity, free spirit, adventure

  REVERSED
  Fear has a voice here, and it sounds reasonable. But notice: is this
  caution protecting you, or is it the voice of old wounds convincing you
  that you are not ready, not worthy, not enough? What story are you telling
  yourself about why now is not the time? What if that story is wrong? What
  would the next smallest step look like, one so small that fear cannot
  catch you?

  keywords: recklessness, fearfulness, risk-taking, inconsideration


THE MAGICIAN  [major-1]

  UPRIGHT
  Everything you need is already within reach. The question is not what you
  lack, but what you have forgotten you possess. What resources are already
  in your hands? What skills have you stopped seeing? Notice the power that
  lives in your voice, your hands, your focused intention. Where in your
  life could you stop wishing and start working with what is already here?

  keywords: manifestation, resourcefulness, power, action, concentration

  REVERSED
  There is a performance happening, and you might be both the magician and
  the audience. Where are you manipulating rather than manifesting? What are
  you trying to force into being through sheer will instead of allowing it
  to unfold? Notice where you are pretending to have it together. What if
  not knowing is the first honest step toward real power?

  keywords: manipulation, cunning, trickery, wasted talent


THE HIGH PRIESTESS  [major-2]

  UPRIGHT
  There is something you already know but have not yet let yourself
  acknowledge. What truth have you been avoiding? What does your body know
  that your mind refuses to admit? Close your eyes and listen, not to logic,
  but to the quiet voice beneath it. What is your intuition trying to tell
  you?

  keywords: intuition, mystery, subconscious, higher power, inner voice

  REVERSED
  Either secrets are being kept, from you or by you, or you have lost
  connection to your inner knowing. Are you ignoring your gut because it
  tells you something inconvenient? What truth are you keeping from
  yourself? If your intuition could speak without fear of judgment, what
  would it say?

  keywords: secrets, disconnection, silence, repressed feelings


THE EMPRESS  [major-3]

  UPRIGHT
  There is a creative force in you that cannot be rushed. Where in your life
  are you being called to create, to nurture, to let something grow at its
  own pace? What needs your gentle, consistent attention? Notice how
  abundance comes not from forcing, but from tending. What are you gestating
  right now that deserves your patience?

  keywords: abundance, nurturing, fertility, nature, beauty

  REVERSED
  Creative energy is blocked, smothered by perfectionism or starved by
  neglect. Where have you abandoned your own care? What part of you is
  withering from lack of attention? If you treated yourself the way you
  treat the things you love, what would change? What one act of
  self-nurturing could you offer yourself today?

  keywords: creative block, dependence, smothering, neglect


THE EMPEROR  [major-4]

  UPRIGHT
  This is about healthy boundaries, systems that serve, leadership that
  protects rather than controls. Where in your life do you need to establish
  order? What boundaries would create safety? What foundation needs
  strengthening? How can you lead yourself with both strength and
  compassion?

  keywords: authority, structure, control, fatherhood, leadership

  REVERSED
  Structure has become rigidity. Control has become domination. Notice where
  power is being wielded to protect fear rather than create safety. Where
  are you being too rigid? What rules are you following that no longer
  serve? If you softened just a little, not collapsed, but softened, what
  might grow? Where has discipline become punishment, of yourself or others?

  keywords: domination, tyranny, rigidity, coldness


THE HIEROPHANT  [major-5]

  UPRIGHT
  There is wisdom in lineage, in learning from those who walked this path
  already. What traditions ground you? What teachings do you want to carry
  forward? But notice also: which traditions serve your growth, and which
  are just familiar? What wisdom from your lineage, biological or chosen, do
  you want to honor?

  keywords: tradition, conformity, spirituality, education, institutions

  REVERSED
  Tradition has become dogma. Teaching has become control. You are being
  called to question what you have been told to believe. Where are you
  following rules that were never yours to begin with? What belief system
  needs to be examined? What would you believe if no one had told you what
  to believe? Where are you ready to become your own authority?

  keywords: rebellion, subversiveness, new approaches, freedom


THE LOVERS  [major-6]

  UPRIGHT
  This is about union, yes, but also about choice. Who are you choosing?
  What relationship is calling you into deeper alignment? Notice that true
  partnership requires you to bring your whole self. What does healthy
  partnership look like to you? What are you willing to offer? What do you
  need in return?

  keywords: love, harmony, relationships, choices, values

  REVERSED
  There is misalignment here, either with another or with yourself. Values
  are clashing, or you have abandoned yourself in service of union. Where
  have you compromised your values for connection? What part of yourself did
  you hide to be loved? What choice have you been avoiding? If this
  relationship ended tomorrow, who would you be?

  keywords: disharmony, imbalance, misalignment, self-love


THE CHARIOT  [major-7]

  UPRIGHT
  Forward motion requires total commitment. You have what it takes, but it
  demands focused will. What are you moving toward? What opposing forces
  need to be harnessed rather than fought? Where do you need to trust your
  own direction, even when others doubt? What goal requires your complete
  focus right now?

  keywords: control, willpower, victory, determination, action

  REVERSED
  Energy is scattered, or you are forcing instead of flowing. You might be
  paralyzed by indecision or charging forward without clear direction. Where
  are you spinning your wheels? What are you trying to control that cannot
  be controlled? What would happen if you chose one clear direction and
  committed fully? What are you avoiding by staying scattered?

  keywords: opposition, lack of direction, aggression, powerlessness


STRENGTH  [major-8]

  UPRIGHT
  This is not about conquering your wildness, but about meeting it with
  compassion. What part of you feels untamed, uncontrollable? What would it
  mean to approach your fear, your anger, your desire with tenderness
  instead of force? Where in your life could softness be more powerful than
  dominance? What part of yourself needs your gentle attention?

  keywords: courage, inner power, patience, compassion, gentle control

  REVERSED
  Where are you either out of control or over-controlling? What would it
  look like to find the middle ground, to let yourself feel without being
  consumed? Where have you mistaken suppression for strength? What part of
  you is begging to be acknowledged?

  keywords: self-doubt, weakness, raw emotion, lack of control


THE HERMIT  [major-9]

  UPRIGHT
  There is a kind of knowing that only comes in solitude. What wisdom are
  you being called to seek? Where do you need to withdraw from the noise to
  hear your own voice? Notice what becomes clear when you stop performing
  for others. What question can only be answered in silence?

  keywords: introspection, solitude, inner guidance, wisdom, soul-searching

  REVERSED
  Isolation has tipped into loneliness, or you are avoiding the world
  because you have mistaken withdrawal for wisdom. Where has solitude become
  hiding? What are you running from by staying alone? Or perhaps you are so
  lost in the noise that you have forgotten how to find yourself. When was
  the last time you sat in silence? What would you hear if you did?

  keywords: isolation, loneliness, withdrawal, avoidance


WHEEL OF FORTUNE  [major-10]

  UPRIGHT
  Cycles turn, and you cannot stop them. Seasons change, luck shifts, what
  was down comes up again. Where in your life are you resisting change that
  is already in motion? What if this shift, even if it feels chaotic, is
  part of a larger pattern you cannot yet see? Notice what is beyond your
  control. What would it mean to trust the movement? Where are you being
  asked to let go?

  keywords: change, cycles, fate, turning point, fortune

  REVERSED
  You are trying to hold things still, or you feel stuck while everything
  around you moves. Where are you clinging to what needs to change? What are
  you resisting that wants to evolve? Notice if you are blaming external
  forces for what is yours to transform. What would shift if you stopped
  fighting the cycle and started working with it?

  keywords: resistance, bad luck, lack of control, stagnation


JUSTICE  [major-11]

  UPRIGHT
  This is about truth, consequence, accountability. What decision are you
  avoiding? Where do you need to act with integrity, even when it is hard?
  Notice that justice is not punishment, it is alignment. What needs to be
  brought back into balance? Where are you being called to take
  responsibility for your choices?

  keywords: fairness, truth, cause and effect, law, accountability

  REVERSED
  There is dishonesty here, either with yourself or with others. Where are
  you being unfair? What truth are you dodging? Notice if you are blaming
  others for consequences you played a part in creating. What would
  accountability look like? What would change if you stopped avoiding the
  truth?

  keywords: unfairness, dishonesty, avoidance, lack of accountability


THE HANGED MAN  [major-12]

  UPRIGHT
  Sometimes you must stop moving to gain perspective. Where in your life are
  you being asked to surrender, to pause, to let go of control? What would
  you see if you looked at this situation from a completely different angle?
  Notice that waiting is not the same as giving up. What insight is only
  available in stillness?

  keywords: suspension, letting go, new perspective, sacrifice, patience

  REVERSED
  You are stuck, but not in the way that brings clarity, stuck in
  resistance, in limbo, in refusing to let go. Where are you holding on when
  you should release? What are you waiting for that is never going to come?
  Notice if you are martyring yourself, suffering without purpose. What
  would shift if you stopped resisting the pause and actually surrendered?

  keywords: stalling, resistance, indecision, martyrdom


DEATH  [major-13]

  UPRIGHT
  Something is ending, and there is no negotiating with it. The old must
  compost to feed the new. What in your life is asking to be released? What
  version of yourself are you being called to let die? Notice that
  transformation is not optional, only your resistance to it is. What would
  you grieve if you let yourself feel it? What could grow in the space left
  behind?

  keywords: endings, transformation, transition, letting go, rebirth

  REVERSED
  You are clinging to what is already dead, or resisting a change that has
  already begun. Where are you refusing to let go? What are you trying to
  keep alive that needs to end? Notice the exhaustion of holding on. What
  would it feel like to stop resisting? What are you afraid will happen if
  you surrender to the inevitable transformation?

  keywords: resistance to change, stagnation, fear of endings, inability to move on


TEMPERANCE  [major-14]

  UPRIGHT
  This is the art of balance, not static, but fluid. Where in your life are
  you being called to blend opposites, to find the middle path? What
  extremes need tempering? Notice how healing happens not through force, but
  through patient integration. What parts of yourself are asking to be
  brought into harmony?

  keywords: balance, moderation, patience, harmony, alchemy

  REVERSED
  Balance has been lost, you are swinging between extremes or forcing
  integration before its time. Where are you being impatient with your own
  process? What are you trying to fix too quickly? Notice if you are
  avoiding discomfort by numbing or distracting. What would it mean to allow
  things to find their own balance? Where do you need to slow down?

  keywords: imbalance, excess, lack of harmony, impatience


THE DEVIL  [major-15]

  UPRIGHT
  What are you bound to that you pretend you cannot escape? What addiction,
  pattern, or relationship keeps you small? Where are you choosing comfort
  over freedom? Notice the seduction of staying stuck. What would you have
  to face if you freed yourself? What are you getting from staying bound?

  keywords: bondage, addiction, materialism, shadow self, attachment

  REVERSED
  You are finally seeing your patterns for what they are. Where are you
  beginning to free yourself from old patterns? What illusion is crumbling?
  Notice if you are afraid of the freedom you are claiming. What would your
  life look like without this attachment? What part of you is ready to walk
  away from what has kept you captive?

  keywords: release, breaking free, awareness, detachment


THE TOWER  [major-16]

  UPRIGHT
  What you built on false ground cannot stand. This is the moment of sudden
  revelation, the breaking down of what was never stable to begin with. What
  illusion is shattering? What structure in your life needs to fall? Notice
  that destruction can be a kind of grace. What truth is being revealed?

  keywords: sudden change, upheaval, revelation, awakening, destruction

  REVERSED
  You are resisting the collapse that needs to happen. Where are you
  propping up something that should crumble? What are you afraid to let
  break? Notice if you are delaying the inevitable. What would it cost you
  to keep rebuilding on faulty ground? What would become possible if you let
  it fall?

  keywords: resistance to change, delayed disaster, fear of change, avoidance


THE STAR  [major-17]

  UPRIGHT
  This is about hope, not as denial, but as the quiet knowing that you will
  survive this. What dream are you being called to tend? Where do you need
  to trust that you are being guided? Notice the light that remains even in
  darkness. What are you healing into?

  keywords: hope, faith, renewal, inspiration, serenity

  REVERSED
  Hope feels far away, or you have lost faith in your own path. Where have
  you disconnected from your sense of purpose? What dream have you given up
  on? Notice if you are comparing your journey to others' and finding
  yourself lacking. What would it mean to return to what feels true for you,
  even if no one else understands it? Where do you need to reconnect with
  wonder?

  keywords: hopelessness, disconnection, lack of faith, discouragement


THE MOON  [major-18]

  UPRIGHT
  The path forward is unclear. You cannot see what is real and what is
  illusion. What fears are surfacing that you have kept hidden? Where is
  your imagination running wild? Notice that not everything needs to be
  clear right now. What is your intuition whispering beneath the anxiety?
  What do you need to trust even when you cannot see?

  keywords: illusion, intuition, uncertainty, subconscious, dreams

  REVERSED
  Clarity is arriving, or you are finally seeing through the illusions that
  have kept you confused. Where have you been deceiving yourself? What truth
  is emerging that you were afraid to face? Notice if clarity is arriving
  and you are still choosing to stay in confusion. What becomes visible when
  you stop hiding from yourself? Where are you ready to see clearly?

  keywords: clarity, truth revealed, release of fear, seeing through illusion


THE SUN  [major-19]

  UPRIGHT
  There is clarity here, warmth, the simple joy of being alive. Where in
  your life can you feel this kind of uncomplicated happiness? What would it
  mean to let yourself be seen, to stop hiding your light? Notice what
  blooms when you stop apologizing for your brightness. Where are you being
  called to celebrate, to play, to trust that things are actually okay?

  keywords: joy, success, vitality, clarity, celebration

  REVERSED
  The light is dimmed, either by self-doubt or by external circumstances you
  cannot control. Where have you lost your sense of joy? What is blocking
  your vitality? Notice if you are downplaying your own success or refusing
  to let yourself feel good. What would it take to believe that you deserve
  to shine? Where do you need to reconnect with simple pleasure?

  keywords: dimmed joy, lack of enthusiasm, self-doubt, blocked vitality


JUDGEMENT  [major-20]

  UPRIGHT
  This is about reckoning, not in punishment, but in awakening. What are you
  being called to answer for? What part of your past needs to be integrated
  before you can move forward? Notice that this is not about shame, but
  about honest reflection. Where do you need to forgive yourself? What are
  you ready to be reborn into?

  keywords: reckoning, awakening, renewal, inner calling, absolution

  REVERSED
  You are avoiding the call, or judging yourself too harshly to hear it.
  Where are you stuck in self-criticism instead of self-awareness? What are
  you refusing to take responsibility for? Notice if you are waiting for
  external validation to give yourself permission to change. What would it
  mean to answer your own call? Where do you need to release judgment and
  choose compassion?

  keywords: self-doubt, harsh judgment, avoidance, inability to forgive


THE WORLD  [major-21]

  UPRIGHT
  The cycle is complete. You have arrived at a place of wholeness,
  integration, achievement. Notice what you have become through this
  journey. What have you learned? What are you ready to celebrate? This is
  not the end, it is the completion of one circle before the next begins.
  Where in your life do you feel this sense of culmination? What are you
  ready to acknowledge in yourself?

  keywords: completion, accomplishment, wholeness, integration, celebration

  REVERSED
  You are close to completion but have not yet crossed the threshold. Where
  are you stopping just before the finish line? What final step are you
  avoiding? Notice if you are sabotaging yourself out of fear of success.
  What would it mean to let yourself arrive? What are you afraid will happen
  if you actually complete this cycle and have to begin again?

  keywords: incompletion, lack of closure, seeking shortcuts, delays

ACE OF CUPS  [cups-ace]

  UPRIGHT
  An offering arrives, love, emotion, creative flow, spiritual connection
  pouring forth. What is your heart opening to? Where are you being invited
  to feel more deeply? Notice that this gift requires you to receive it.
  What would it mean to let yourself be filled? What new emotional beginning
  is available to you?

  keywords: new love, emotional beginning, creative flow, spiritual connection, overflowing feelings

  REVERSED
  You are refusing what is being offered. Where are you blocking your own
  emotional flow? What love or connection are you turning away from? Notice
  if you are protecting yourself from feeling because you have been hurt
  before. What would it cost you to stay closed? What might arrive if you
  allowed yourself to open, even just a little?

  keywords: emotional blockage, repressed feelings, closed heart, emptiness


TWO OF CUPS  [cups-2]

  UPRIGHT
  Partnership, connection, the joy of being truly seen through mutual
  recognition. Where in your life are you experiencing this kind of
  reciprocity? What relationship, romantic, platonic, creative, is asking
  for your attention? Notice that true connection requires both people to
  show up fully. What are you willing to offer? What do you need in return?

  keywords: partnership, mutual attraction, unity, connection, balance in relationships

  REVERSED
  There is imbalance here, one person giving more, one taking more, or both
  of you performing connection without actually feeling it. Where has
  reciprocity been lost? What are you pretending to feel that you do not?
  Notice if you are trying to force intimacy where it does not naturally
  exist. What would honesty look like in this relationship? What would shift
  if you told the truth?

  keywords: imbalance, broken communication, disconnection, tension in relationships


THREE OF CUPS  [cups-3]

  UPRIGHT
  Community, friendship, shared joy calling for celebration. Where in your
  life can you celebrate with others? What success wants to be acknowledged?
  Notice the healing that happens when you let yourself be held by your
  people. Who are your people? What would it mean to let them see you happy?

  keywords: celebration, friendship, community, gathering, joy shared

  REVERSED
  The celebration feels hollow, or you are isolating when you should be
  reaching out. Where have you disconnected from your community? What
  friendship have you neglected? Notice if you are comparing yourself to
  others instead of celebrating with them. What would it cost you to ask for
  support? Where do you need to return to connection?

  keywords: isolation, loneliness, overindulgence, disconnection from community


FOUR OF CUPS  [cups-4]

  UPRIGHT
  You are fixated on dissatisfaction, missing what is being offered. What
  opportunity are you overlooking because you are focused on what is not
  working? Where has familiarity bred contempt? Notice what you are refusing
  to see because you have decided nothing good can come. What is being
  offered that you are ignoring?

  keywords: apathy, contemplation, missed opportunity, dissatisfaction, introspection

  REVERSED
  You are finally seeing what was always there, or you have been stuck in
  apathy for so long that something is shaking you awake. Where are you
  beginning to see what you could not see before? What opportunity are you
  finally ready to notice? What would it mean to let yourself want something
  again? Where do you need to open your eyes?

  keywords: awareness dawning, new perspective, openness to opportunities, motivation returning


FIVE OF CUPS  [cups-5]

  UPRIGHT
  Loss has your full attention, while what remains goes unseen. What are you
  grieving? What disappointment feels unbearable right now? Notice that your
  pain is real and deserves to be felt, but so does the possibility of what
  still stands. Where are you being called to turn around? What is still
  here, waiting for your attention?

  keywords: loss, grief, disappointment, focusing on the negative, mourning

  REVERSED
  You are beginning to turn around, or you are stuck in grief that has
  become an identity. Where are you ready to move forward? What are you
  holding onto because letting go would mean admitting it is really over?
  Notice if your mourning has become a way to avoid living. What would it
  mean to honor what was lost and still choose what remains?

  keywords: acceptance, moving on, finding what remains, recovery, forgiveness


SIX OF CUPS  [cups-6]

  UPRIGHT
  This is about innocence, nostalgia, the sweetness of what was offered in
  gentler times. Where in your life are you being called to reconnect with
  your past? What memory holds healing? Notice that looking back can be
  medicine, but it can also be escape. What gift from your younger self are
  you ready to receive? What would it mean to tend to your inner child?

  keywords: nostalgia, innocence, childhood memories, reunion, simplicity

  REVERSED
  You are either stuck in the past or running from it entirely. Where are
  you clinging to old patterns because they feel safe? What memory are you
  romanticizing in a way that keeps you from living now? Notice if you are
  using nostalgia to avoid the present. What would it mean to honor your
  past without living in it? What is here, now, that needs your attention?

  keywords: living in the past, idealization, inability to move forward, clinging to old ways


SEVEN OF CUPS  [cups-7]

  UPRIGHT
  So many possibilities float before you, each tempting with different
  visions of what could be. Where in your life are you overwhelmed by
  options? What fantasy is distracting you from action? Notice that dreaming
  is necessary, but at some point you must choose. What would it mean to
  commit to one path? What are you avoiding by keeping all doors open?

  keywords: choices, fantasy, illusion, overwhelming options, imagination

  REVERSED
  Clarity is arriving, or you are finally seeing through the illusions that
  kept you paralyzed. Where are you ready to discern between fantasy and
  reality? What dream needs to be released because it was never truly yours?
  Notice if you are still avoiding commitment by pretending you are still
  deciding. What would it mean to choose? What becomes possible when you
  stop chasing every shiny thing?

  keywords: clarity, discernment, grounding, releasing illusions, making a choice


EIGHT OF CUPS  [cups-8]

  UPRIGHT
  What once fulfilled you no longer does, and you are being called to walk
  away. Where in your life are you being called to leave behind what once
  satisfied you but now feels hollow? What are you outgrowing? Notice that
  walking away is not failure, it is the courage to seek what your soul
  truly needs. What are you ready to release? Where is the next mountain
  calling you?

  keywords: walking away, seeking deeper meaning, letting go, soul-searching, moving on

  REVERSED
  You know you should leave, but you are not leaving. Where are you staying
  out of fear, comfort, or the sunk cost of what you have already invested?
  What are you pretending is still working? Notice the cost of staying when
  your soul is already gone. What would it take to trust yourself enough to
  walk away? What are you afraid you will lose if you go?

  keywords: fear of leaving, stagnation, avoidance, staying in comfort, resistance to change


NINE OF CUPS  [cups-9]

  UPRIGHT
  Wishes fulfilled, deep satisfaction earned and deserved. What have you
  achieved that deserves celebration? Where in your life can you feel this
  kind of contentment? Notice that you are allowed to enjoy what you have
  earned. What would it mean to let yourself feel satisfied? What wish has
  already come true that you have not yet acknowledged?

  keywords: satisfaction, wishes fulfilled, contentment, emotional fulfillment, gratitude

  REVERSED
  You have what you thought you wanted, but it does not feel the way you
  expected. Where is the victory hollow? What achievement has not brought
  the happiness you were promised? Notice if you are chasing external
  markers of success while ignoring your internal emptiness. What do you
  actually want, beneath what you think you are supposed to want? What would
  true satisfaction feel like?

  keywords: dissatisfaction, hollow victory, unfulfilled wishes, materialism, complacency


TEN OF CUPS  [cups-10]

  UPRIGHT
  Emotional wholeness, not just happiness, but deep belonging and lasting
  harmony. Where in your life do you feel truly at home? What relationships
  nourish you at the deepest level? Notice that this kind of joy is built
  through consistent care. What community are you part of? What would it
  mean to let yourself believe that this kind of love is possible?

  keywords: harmony, family, happiness, emotional fulfillment, lasting joy

  REVERSED
  The ideal feels far away, or the family structure is fractured. Where has
  harmony been disrupted? What relationship have you idealized in a way that
  keeps you from seeing the real work it requires? Notice if you are blaming
  others for your own unhappiness. What would it mean to grieve the family
  you wish you had and tend to the one you actually have? Where do you need
  to choose connection over perfection?

  keywords: broken family, disconnection, shattered ideals, misalignment in relationships


PAGE OF CUPS  [cups-page]

  UPRIGHT
  The messenger of the heart arrives with something soft: a creative
  impulse, an intuitive nudge, an invitation to feel something you haven't
  let yourself feel yet. What is your heart quietly asking for right now?
  Where are you being called to sit with your feelings instead of analyzing
  them away? Notice the playfulness in this card. What would it mean to
  approach your inner world with wonder instead of judgment?

  keywords: creative beginnings, intuitive messages, gentle emotions, curiosity, sensitivity

  REVERSED
  Emotions are immature, or you are ignoring the messages your heart is
  sending. Where are you being emotionally avoidant? What creative impulse
  are you dismissing as silly or impractical? Notice if you are performing
  feelings you do not actually have. What would it mean to take your own
  inner world seriously? Where do you need to grow up emotionally, or soften
  into vulnerability?

  keywords: emotional immaturity, creative blocks, ignored intuition, escapism


KNIGHT OF CUPS  [cups-knight]

  UPRIGHT
  The romantic, the dreamer, the one who follows their heart without apology
  and offers devotion freely. What are you being called toward that feels
  like magic? What creative or romantic pursuit is asking for your devotion?
  Notice that not everything needs to be logical. Where do you need to trust
  the pull of beauty? What would it mean to let yourself be enchanted?

  keywords: romance, following your heart, creativity, charm, idealism

  REVERSED
  The dream has become escapism, or the romance is all performance. Where
  are you chasing an idealized version of something rather than seeing what
  is actually in front of you? What are you in love with, the idea or the
  reality? Notice if you are all talk and no action. What would it look like
  to ground your dreams without losing their magic? Where do you need to
  move from fantasy into embodied choice?

  keywords: unrealistic expectations, moodiness, empty promises, fantasy over reality, jealousy


QUEEN OF CUPS  [cups-queen]

  UPRIGHT
  Emotional fluency, the ability to hold your own feelings and the feelings
  of others without losing yourself, staying calm and deeply feeling. Where
  are you being called to lead with compassion? What situation needs your
  tender, steady presence? Notice that true strength is not about being
  unshakeable, it is about feeling everything and staying rooted. How can
  you honor your sensitivity as a gift?

  keywords: compassion, emotional maturity, intuition, nurturing, calm presence

  REVERSED
  Boundaries have dissolved, or you have shut down entirely. Where are you
  so focused on caring for others that you have forgotten yourself? What are
  you absorbing that is not yours to carry? Notice if you are drowning in
  emotion, your own or others'. What would it mean to nurture yourself with
  the same tenderness you offer everyone else? Where do you need to reclaim
